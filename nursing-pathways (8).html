For the new nurse

Your first steps, in order.

Whether you're still choosing a program or you just passed your boards, these are the moves that build a strong, flexible foundation. You don't have to know your final destination yet, you just have to take the next right step.

1

Choose your entry route

Pick an accredited ADN (about 2 years) or BSN (about 4 years) program. A BSN opens more doors and is increasingly preferred by hospitals, but ADN-to-BSN bridges let you start sooner and finish later.

2

Pass the NCLEX-RN

After graduating, apply for licensure through your state board, register with Pearson VUE, and pass the NCLEX-RN. This single exam turns your degree into an active license to practice.

3

Get your safety certs

Earn BLS (Basic Life Support) right away; most employers also want ACLS and sometimes PALS within your first months. These are short courses, easy wins, and often required to apply.

4

Land your first role

Look for a nurse residency or new-grad program. Many nurses start in med-surg or telemetry to build broad assessment skills, a foundation that transfers to almost any specialty later.

5

Find a mentor

Ask a seasoned nurse, charge nurse, or educator to check in with you. A good mentor shortens your learning curve and helps you read the unwritten rules of the unit.

6

Join a professional body

Membership in the ANA or a specialty organization gives you CE discounts, job boards, conferences, and a network long before you need to lean on it.

A realistic first three years

Months 0–6
Survive and stabilize. Finish orientation, master your unit's routines and charting, and focus on safe, confident fundamentals. This is normal-hard for everyone.
Months 6–12
Find your footing. Take a fuller patient load, start noticing which patients and problems energize you. Begin a simple journal of cases that fascinate you.
Year 1–2
Choose a direction. With about a year of experience you qualify for many specialty certifications. Pick one area and go deep, or try a transfer to test a specialty.
Year 2–3
Build toward the next tier. Earn a specialty certification, finish an RN-to-BSN if needed, and explore whether advanced practice, leadership, or teaching calls to you.

A two-minute orientation

How to use this app.

Everything here lives in your browser and works offline. There's no account, no sign-up, and nothing you do is sent anywhere. Here's the quickest way to get value from it, then a tour of each tab.

Quick start

Four steps from opening the app to a printed plan.

1

Set the mood

Pick one of the six pastel palettes using the dots labeled Palette in the top bar. Your choice is remembered next time you open the app.

2

Explore

Browse career paths and tap any role to see credentials, timelines, and first steps. Found one that excites you? Use Set as my goal.

3

Plan

Open My action plan, fill in your details, and add milestones with target dates. It auto-fills the goal if you set one while exploring.

4

Save & print

Press Save plan to keep it in this browser, then Download PDF report for a clean copy you can keep, print, or share.

What each tab does

The numbers match the tabs along the top.

01

Explore pathways

A visual map of how nursing careers tend to flow, followed by detailed cards for sixteen roles.

  • Use the filter chips (All paths, Foundations, Registered Nurse, and so on) to narrow the cards by stage.
  • Tap See what it takes on any card to open credentials, typical time, ordered first steps, and resource links.
  • Tap Set as my goal in action plan to send that role straight to your plan and jump to it.
02

Getting started

For a new or future nurse: the first moves that build a strong, flexible foundation.

  • Read the six first steps in order, from choosing a program to joining a professional body.
  • Use the first-three-years timeline to set realistic expectations for early practice.
03

Resources

Trusted organizations grouped by purpose, from licensure to scholarships.

  • Each link opens in a new tab, so you won't lose your place or your plan.
  • Bookmark the groups tied to your path, and always confirm details with your state board of nursing.
04

My action plan

The interactive part: capture where you are, where you're headed, and the steps between.

  • Fill in About you and Your goal. The goal menu includes every pathway, or arrives pre-filled from the Explore tab.
  • Under Milestones, press Add a milestone to create a row, then set its category, target month, and status. The × removes a row.
  • List three next actions you can start this week, the small wins that build momentum.
  • Save plan stores it in this browser · Clear empties everything (it asks first) · Download PDF report makes the printable copy.

Getting your PDF report

Press Download PDF report to build a polished, designed one-to-two-page report of your plan. It will download directly, or open in a new browser tab where you can read it and use your browser's own Print or Save button. Either way you get a clean copy to keep or share — the rest of the app is never included.

Where your plan is kept

Saving stores your plan only in this browser on this device, so it's waiting when you return. It won't appear on another computer, and clearing your browser data removes it. For a permanent copy, download the PDF report. If you're using a shared or private-mode browser where saving is blocked, the app will tell you and the PDF is your best way to keep your work.

A note on accuracy. Timeframes and credential names are typical for the United States and vary by state, school, and employer. Always verify current requirements with your state board of nursing and your chosen program.
